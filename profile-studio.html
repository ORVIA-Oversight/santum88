Beginning

Start with what is true.

What does a normal week look like? What makes you laugh? What do you care about? What do your friends know about you that a dating profile usually misses?

Mara can help you reflect on the story you are telling, but she does not invent a personality for you or diagnose what kind of person you are.

Your dating team

Mara helps with clarity. Cal helps with the next practical step.

They do not impersonate you, conduct romantic conversations on your behalf or make consent decisions.
