Included private chat

When we introduce you, talking to that match is included.

Many dating services reserve useful messaging features for extra charges or higher tiers. LifeMatch takes a simpler position: if your subscription includes matching, private chat with the people we match you with is part of that service.

No message packs. No per-message fees. No artificial unlock just to continue the introduction.

The chat is match-only, not a public community inbox. You talk to the specific people LifeMatch has introduced you to, with filters for active, paused and closed matches.
