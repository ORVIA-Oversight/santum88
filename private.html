Private standard

Discretion without
ambiguity.

The more private the environment, the clearer the rules must be.

Consent

Freely given, specific, current and reversible. Preferences, payment, membership, matching, screening and room entry are never consent.

Privacy

No public member directory. No photography by default. Identity and age checks are private and controlled.

Staff boundaries

On-duty staff and security host, protect and respond. They do not participate sexually with guests or supply partners.

Welfare first

Doubt over capacity, coercion, serious intoxication, distress or medical risk means stop and respond.

Private does not mean outside the law. Sanctum 88 is designed to operate lawfully, subject to specialist legal, licensing, venue and insurance approval.
This page is a public summary, not the complete operating manual, risk assessment, licensing position or consent policy.
