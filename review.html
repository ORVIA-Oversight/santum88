THE LIFEMATCH POSITION

We don’t decide what your relationship should look like. You do.

Straight, gay, lesbian, bi, pan, queer, monogamous, polyamorous, divorced, widowed, single parent, disabled, neurodivergent, religious, artistic, outdoorsy, homebody, thirty or seventy. The starting point is the person — not a category.

NOT JUST A DATING APP

A guided route from “I want something” to “we should meet”.

We are building the layer that ordinary dating apps often leave to you: clarity, presentation, verification, communication insight, compatibility reasoning, preparation and movement into real life.

ONE SERVICE · MANY REAL LIVES

Whoever you are, the matching logic starts with your reality.

LGBTQ+

Same-sex, bi, pan and queer dating treated as ordinary dating — not a side category.

Ethical non-monogamy

Polyamorous and open relationship preferences can be stated clearly where all adults knowingly opt in.

Single parents

Children, time, routines, co-parenting realities and family plans belong in the compatibility picture.

Disability & accessibility

Accessible venues, sensory needs, communication preferences and practical support can be part of date planning without reducing someone to a diagnosis.

Faith & culture

Religion, tradition, family expectations and cultural life can matter greatly — so they can matter in matching.

Later life

Companionship, romance, travel and new chapters do not have an expiry date.

Creative & alternative lives

Artists, makers, free spirits, outdoors people and unconventional lifestyles need someone who understands the rhythm of that life.

Still figuring it out

You do not have to arrive with the perfect label or relationship plan. Clarity can be part of the journey.
