SAFETY, PRIVACY & HONESTY

You should know what is happening with your information.

The production platform will use product-scoped permissions, clear privacy choices and visible reporting/blocking controls. This page is a launch-design statement, not a substitute for final legal notices.

Your data

Identity information, Match compatibility information and optional sensitive preferences should be separated, permissioned and minimised.

Consent

Joining, matching, messaging, meeting or paying never creates consent to another person. Consent remains specific, current and reversible.

Block and report

Production must make these controls obvious and available from every conversation and member profile.

No hidden scoring

Members should see why an introduction is being made rather than receive an unexplained compatibility percentage.
