BEGINNING · TRUST FIRST

Before an introduction feels exciting, it has to feel safe enough to consider.

Safety is not one badge. It is a chain of decisions: identity, privacy, consent, controls, reporting and honest limits on what technology can know.

Beginning
✓

Verified Human

Identity and age checks are designed to reduce impersonation, duplicate accounts and obvious fraud signals.

◉

Private identity

Verification status can be visible without exposing raw ID documents, liveness data or unnecessary personal information.

↺

Consent stays current

Joining, matching, messaging, meeting or paying never creates consent to anything else.

Identity separation

Your legal identity should not become your dating profile.

The intended architecture separates verification data from member profile, matching, safety and audit information. Mara, Cal and matching logic should only receive the minimum status needed — for example, that a person is verified — rather than raw ID or biometric material.

Final verification provider, retention rules and privacy notices require formal launch approval.

AI guardrails

No diagnosis

Mara and Cal are dating guides, not therapists, clinicians or diagnostic tools.

No manipulation

No coercive seduction advice, bypassing refusal or tricks designed to pressure another person.

No secret profiling

No hidden desirability score, ranking of human worth or covert profiling of another person.

No autonomous romance

AI does not impersonate you, make consent decisions or conduct romantic or sexual relationships on your behalf.

Controls

Blocking, reporting and escalation must be obvious.

The production service is intended to make block and report controls available from matched conversations and profiles, with moderation and escalation where required.

This is a required launch capability, not a claim that every production moderation workflow is already verified today.

End

The system can reduce risk. People still make the decisions.

Technology can support safer introductions, but members remain responsible for boundaries, consent and real-world choices. LifeMatch is designed to help — not take over.
