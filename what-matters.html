WHAT MATTERS

The things most dating profiles leave until later.

Compatibility is not one score. It is a collection of real-world fits, differences and conversations.

Life direction

Relationship intent, family plans, geography, career, ambition and future change.

Everyday life

Sleep, tidiness, routines, pets, food, social energy, money and time.

Communication

Directness, reassurance, conflict, processing time, affection and repair.

Adventure

Travel, spontaneity, weekends, outdoors, comfort, luxury and budget.

Attraction & intimacy

Private, optional and handled with care. Attraction matters, but it is not the whole match.

Boundaries

Preferences, strong preferences, areas of compromise and genuine dealbreakers.
