Different is not always wrong

Some differences are complementary. Some need a proper conversation.

LifeMatch should not turn ordinary human difference into failure. One person may plan while another is spontaneous. One may love travel while the other needs more home time. The useful question is whether the difference works in real life.

We do not decide what your relationship should look like. You do.
