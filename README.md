## Important status notes
- Pricing shown on the homepage is explicitly a proposed founding test, not a final approved tariff.
- Onboarding is a non-storing prototype.
- Trust/privacy statements describe required production behaviour and should be replaced/supplemented with approved legal notices before full data collection.
- Sanctum Private remains separate from Match.
