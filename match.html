BEGINNING · AN INTRODUCTION, NOT A VERDICT

Why do we think these two people may be worth introducing?

Because enough of real life appears to fit — and because the differences look discussable rather than hidden.

Beginning

What looks aligned

Strong alignmentFamily plans, outdoor weekends, communication style and desire for a committed relationship.
Different but potentially complementaryOne is more organised; one is more spontaneous.
Worth talking aboutTravel spending and how much social time each person enjoys.
UnknownWe do not fill gaps with guesses. If something important is not known yet, we say so.

“This is not a prediction. It is a reason to have a conversation.”

Middle

Then the profile stops mattering as much.

The point of matching is to get two suitable adults into a real conversation. Chemistry, humour, comfort and curiosity can only be discovered by people themselves.

If both people want to continue, matched chat is included in the subscription. There is no extra message pack or unlock charge.

End

If it feels worth exploring, meet. If it doesn’t, don’t.

No pressure, no hidden penalty, no system deciding what you should feel.
